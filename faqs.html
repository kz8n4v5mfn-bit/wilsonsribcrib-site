Frequently Asked Questions

What are your hours?

Tuesday through Saturday, 11 AM to 8 PM, and Sunday, 11 AM to 6 PM. We're closed Monday. Some days we sell out early, so call ahead if you're coming late.

What are your holiday hours?

We're closed Thanksgiving Day (Thursday, November 26) and Christmas Day (Friday, December 25). Every other day we keep our regular hours. Holiday orders for whole slabs, family packs, turkey legs and whole cakes need 24–48 hours' notice: call (601) 600-2216.

Where are you located?

220 E Presley Blvd, McComb, MS 39648. There's free parking in our lot and on the street.

Can I call ahead?

Yes. Call (601) 600-2216 and your order will be ready when you get here.

Do you have curbside pickup?

Yes. Call ahead, tell us your car, and we'll bring it out.

Do you deliver?

Yes, through DoorDash and Uber Eats. We don't run our own delivery drivers. Tap Order Now on any page to order on DoorDash, or find us on Uber Eats.

Can I eat in?

No. We're takeout only, with no dine-in seating. Order pickup online at order.wilsonsribcrib.com, call ahead, pull up curbside, or order delivery on DoorDash or Uber Eats.

Do you have a drive-through?

No, we don't have a drive-through. Call ahead or use curbside.

What forms of payment do you take?

All major credit cards, debit cards, tap to pay and cash. We don't take checks.

Are ribs wet or dry?

Your choice. Every rib plate and rib tip plate comes wet (sauced) or dry rub.

Do the plates come with a drink?

No. Drinks are sold separately. Plates come with two sides and a fresh roll; combos come with one side.

Do you cater?

Yes. Family packs feed the house, and for bigger groups send us a catering request and we'll call you back.

Is the building wheelchair accessible?

Yes. The entrance and parking are wheelchair accessible.

Are you hiring?

We're always glad to meet good people. Fill out the form on our Careers page.

Still have a question? Contact us or call (601) 600-2216.

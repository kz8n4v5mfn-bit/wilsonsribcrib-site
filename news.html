Know your ribs

Wet or Dry? How We Do Ribs

Dry rub means the seasoning and the smoke do all the talking. You get a dark, peppery bark and a clean bite.

Wet means we finish the same ribs with our BBQ sauce, so they're sticky, sweet and messy in the best way.

Can't pick? Get rib tips one way and ribs the other. Every rib plate and rib tip plate can go either way.

Our story

New Name. Same Slow Smoke.

The Wilson family has been smoking ribs in McComb since 2010. In 2026 we reopened as Wilsons Rib Crib & Express with a fresh look and the same slow-smoked flavor.

Find us at 220 E Presley Blvd, Tuesday through Saturday.

Local guide

Where to Get Slow-Smoked BBQ in McComb, MS

Looking for barbecue in McComb? Wilsons Rib Crib & Express is at 220 E Presley Blvd, open Tuesday through Saturday from 11 AM to 8 PM. We smoke ribs, rib tips, chicken leg quarters, sausage and turkey legs every day we're open.
